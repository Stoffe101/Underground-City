# Cities, world, territory, and resources

## Region structure

A **finite curated region** containing three or four significant rival municipalities, connecting roads, smaller hamlets, rivers/mountains, farmland, industrial districts, resource sites, and disputed corridors. Starting cities are selectable on first join. City names and specific counts are provisional.

World assembly candidates: (A) customize The Lost Cities and author important districts; (B) adapt one existing connected modern-city map; (C) import permitted schematics/regions into a base world. Selection follows a visual, legal, performance and NPC-pathing spike.

## Territory and travel

- Region boundaries defined by named polygons/volumes in data; avoid hard-coded coordinates in logic.
- Roads/rails/vehicle routes and guarded border crossings should offer meaningful journey choices.
- Different security rules in core residential areas, industrial zones, borderlands, criminal territories and wilderness.
- Prioritize compact travel distances over pure size. Buildings should be explorable, navigable and populated where gameplay requires it.

## Resources and mining

Move primary resources into designated mines, open quarries, exposed mountain deposits, industrial salvage yards, and dangerous contested sites. Restrict or redesign vanilla deep-strip-mining progression; no need to rush diamonds/enchantments as the main game loop. Resource nodes need finite capacities or rate limits, transparent refresh rules, and anti-exploit protections. City-owned mines may require permits/contracts.

## Settlement growth

Treasury + deliverable materials + story decisions contribute to predefined development projects. At completion, swap/activate an approved schematic or region, NPC schedules, shops, services and defenses. Never paste over player-owned structures without explicit validation and backup.
