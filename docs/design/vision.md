# Vision and design pillars

**Working genre (updated October 18, 2026, [D-080](../production/decisions.md)):** a **modern open-world Minecraft RPG sandbox** for a private pair of friends (room for more). Three inspirations, none of them a licence to copy protected material: **GTA** (living cities, vehicles, robberies, police, property, businesses, many ways to commit a crime), **Schedule I** (start small, build production operations, craft and sell valuable products, grow a co-operative operation with friends) and **DeceasedCraft** (urban detail, backpacks, food and water, looting, atmosphere). It is **not** a zombie conversion: the world is populated and functional. "Semi-modern" remains the description of what Lost Cities actually generates.

**The central loop:** explore, acquire materials, components and blueprints, craft or grow or process, sell to real buyers, earn money, XP and reputation, unlock skills and equipment, improve your home and workshop, expand. **Crafting and manufacturing are central**: real components wherever the chosen mods have them; loot complements production and never replaces it; other careers may own firearms, and the Gunsmith's edge is *production*, not permission. **Survival is a pillar**, balanced for a functioning modern society (food, water and supplies can be bought in cities; preparation is rewarded on long journeys). Every career has a **legal path and an underground path** open to the same character at all times (D-049). **Crews** (a shared bank, shared homes, never merged careers) are a major *optional* feature ([crews.md](crews.md)).

**Five careers:** Farmer, Vintner (two careers, D-067), Smith/Engineer, Gunsmith/Arms Dealer, Hacker/Technician.

**Hard exclusion:** the **Create** mod, its add-ons and anything that requires it are not part of this project and must not be added, proposed as a dependency or used as a rebinding example.

## Player fantasy

Begin as an ordinary new citizen. Choose a home city and livelihood. Earn trust, develop a workshop/farm/business, learn specialized techniques, influence local projects, discover political secrets, and eventually help determine the region's future. The world changes because of participation, not only because of combat kills.

## Non-negotiable pillars

1. **Alive without a crowd of humans.** NPC citizens must have believable roles, schedules, demands and relationships, while off-screen simulation stays affordable.
2. **Every livelihood matters.** Peaceful and criminal careers both have viable loops. No single class is required to complete the cooperative campaign.
3. **Economics have material consequences.** Currency, tax, wages, production, supplies and city projects are connected. Avoid infinity shops and currency duplication.
4. **Choice changes the world.** Reputation, missions, restored neighborhoods, relationships, and diplomacy must persist.
5. **Combat is dangerous but not omnipresent.** Hostile territory, contraband and policing create tension; safe districts and deliberate conflicts protect peaceful play.
6. **A believable physical place.** Dense DeceasedCraft-inspired urban variety, furnished usable interiors, rural production, exposed resources, roads and bounded city territories; no open-ended vanilla diamond grind.
7. **Low friction for a private pair.** No offline punishment, no forced PvP, no requirement for 7+ real players, configurable difficulty and co-op assistance.

## What this is not

Not a typical kitchen-sink modpack, not a zombie survival conversion, not a public MMO or pay-to-win server, not vanilla Minecraft progression with extra jobs, and not a promise of fully simulated thousands of ticking entities.

## Experience targets (proposed)

- A new player can register, find home and job, and fulfill a first order without external tutorials.
- Both players can pursue different careers in parallel and still cooperate on high-value decisions.
- Police react to evidence and law, not omniscience. A criminal mission is a choice, not mandatory.
- Every important city has recognizable infrastructure, leadership, economy, and cultural identity.
- After substantial play, some physical and systemic world state differs meaningfully from day one.

## Inspiration vs ownership

Tale of Kingdoms influences settlement/influence progression. DeceasedCraft influences urban scale and district variety. Neither game's protected maps/assets/scripts are authorized for copying by that inspiration. Read the [third-party policy](../legal/third-party-policy.md).
