# Law, crime, security, and regional conflict

## Core goals

Consequences should be predictable and fair. Legitimate businesses are always viable. Criminal work is **optional high-risk/high-reward** within fictional gameplay.

## Crimes and evidence

Each municipality defines a data-driven legal policy: prohibited items/activities, permitted licenses, protected zones, fine bands, warrants and response rules. **Possession** is not magically detected; guards need a witnessed offense, lawful checkpoint inspection, an NPC report, discovered contraband, or an active warrant.

Proposed heat stages: suspicion → questioning/inspection → pursuit → arrest/warrant → cooldown/reconciliation. Stealth, reputations and witness credibility may influence detection, but avoid unfair random arrests.

## Arrest flow

A guard confronts a suspect, gives an understandable reason, and offers surrender/intervention. On validated arrest, confiscate **only legally defined contraband/evidence**, issue bounded fine or brief in-game holding consequence, and update reputation. Track inventory before/after to prevent loss of unrelated equipment. Make recovery and appeal/reduction pathways possible.

## Weapons and combat

TaCZ is a possible firearm basis. Separate civilian gun ownership, licensed gunsmith work, police/military authorization and illegal black-market sales. Ammo and armor are valuable economic goods; avoid universal access to top-tier weapon loot. Create safe-zone constraints, PvP opt-in decisions, and friendly-fire settings for a private pair.

## Faction relations and warfare

Cities have relationships that can shift through story acts, player action and economy. Hostility is conditional on territory, uniform/allegiance, trespassing, aggression, local laws and NPC faction membership. Wars should be **controlled events with defined objectives**, not endless random attacks that ruin farm/business sessions.

Examples: border convoy ambush, guard-post defense, smuggler crackdown, neighborhood recapture, ceasefire negotiations. Defeated patrols and destroyed structures must recover without permanently corrupting the map.

## Required tests

No false positive arrests; contraband-only confiscation; consistent fine and wanted-level persistence; safe-zone protection; no unavoidable two-player death-loop; guard vs bandit discrimination; performance while NPCs use weapons.
