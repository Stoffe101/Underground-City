# Sprint report, October 17, 2026: the living world, profession depth and a production-quality visual overhaul

Branch `feature/living-world-visual-overhaul`, draft PR [#22](https://github.com/Stoffe101/Underground-City/pull/22) into `feature/professions-vehicles-firearms-sprint` (PR [#21](https://github.com/Stoffe101/Underground-City/pull/21), still open and unmerged at the start of this sprint, head `6e5d6de`). Nothing is merged by this work. Everything is built and tested in Claude's cloud container and on GitHub-hosted runners, never on the owner's computer.

**Status vocabulary** is the one of the [October 16 report](sprint-2026-10-16.md): *implemented and tested* names the kind of test; *scripted-player check* means a real client driven by genuine input events (proves the chain, not feel); *frame viewed* means a screenshot was opened and looked at. **No human has played any of this.**

## A. Executive summary

**The short version.** Underground City now has one visual identity instead of default Minecraft grey: a generated art pipeline, one interface toolkit and every custom screen redesigned on it (registration, Operations Terminal, Public Ledger, conversations, notices, the trace, the order tracker), finished item, crop and block art, a four-stage Sable Leaf, a **fifth career** (Vintner, the owner's Farmer/Vintner split, D-067), a populated Founders Row v2 with a Public Ledger, a firing range and twelve citizens you can talk to while their AI runs, a **server-side blueprint gate** at TaCZ's Gun Smith Table, a **search of Immersive Vehicles' crates** at checkpoints, and real wine, crop and spirits mods in an opt-in dev-pack set.

**What I would tell the owner first.**

1. *The Farmer/Vintner split is built, not just planned.* Farmer = field crops, greenhouses and the restricted Sable Leaf; Vintner = wine, cellars and, underground, a still. Five trades now appear on the registration screen, in the Public Ledger, in the skill trees, in 8 Vintner orders (5 need Vinery or Kaleidoscope Tavern and hide when they are absent) and in Founders Row (a Cellar Master's office and a vineyard). The AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md career lists still say "Farmer/Vintner" for one career; I have **not** edited them (section O, P0).
2. *Existing mods were preferred, with evidence.* [Let's Do] Vinery 1.4.41, [Let's Do] Farm & Charm 1.0.14 and Kaleidoscope Tavern 1.2.0 (vodka, rum, whiskey by barrel) are pinned as opt-in sets `vintner` and `farming`, boot on a dedicated server and are photographed in a real client. Tadacko's Drinks (real stills) crashes a dedicated server and was dropped; Smokeleaf (the only cannabis mod with a 1.20.1 Forge file) is not realistic and needs a Forge bump, so the Sable Leaf stays original art (section E). Interface libraries were evaluated and none replaces our toolkit (section C, D-069).
3. *The first real-client QA of the new screens found real bugs that the old checks had missed*, among them skills that were **drawn on top of each other and invisible** (two nodes in one column and tier were stacked, in every career's tree), notices that never appeared in the capture, and registration cards that overflowed. They are fixed and viewed (section C).
4. *A silent failure was found and fixed:* the TaCZ blueprint gate's mixin had never been loaded, because Forge 1.20.1 does not read `[[mixins]]` from `mods.toml`. The gate now loads (`--mixin.config` in development runs, `MixinConfigs` in the jar manifest) and its GameTest passes.
5. *What is still missing is mostly play, not code:* no human has played, driven or shot anything; roadside content, road signage, boat customs, vehicle fuel and a longer first half hour were not touched (section O).

**Branch, PR, commit, CI.** Head at the time of writing: see the PR; hosted CI results are in section M.

## B. Visual direction

One short document, [visual-identity.md](../design/visual-identity.md): a dark slate-blue interface with a **teal legal** accent, a **red underground** accent and **amber** for money and warnings, a pixel display font for titles and the game's own font for words, one icon sheet of 32 glyphs, and the five trades each with a colour (Farmer green, Vintner violet, Smith copper, Gunsmith brass, Technician cyan) and an icon. The same colours mean the same things everywhere: a legal order is teal, an off-the-books one red, a prize amber. Every colour, border and plate is **generated** by `tools/art` and checked in CI (D-070), so nothing is hand-edited pixels that cannot be reproduced.

## E. Sable Leaf

*Decision:* keep the original crop and be honest about why. The Modrinth search has no cannabis, hemp or moonshine project for Forge 1.20.1 at all. The only cannabis mod with a 1.20.1 Forge file (Smokeleaf Industries, CurseForge only, MIT code, art licence unstated) draws one flat 16 x 16 sprite in 25 tints, has no palmate leaf, brings a second trading and effects system and needs Forge 47.4.23 where we are pinned to 47.4.10 (D-007). The rest could not be read from the container.

*What was built:* a crop made of real **element models**, not a flat sprite: a node-ringed stem and **palmate leaves** (five to nine serrated leaflets that open as the plant grows), a flowering top with orange pistils on the last stage, four growth stages plus a bale and a cutting (the seed), all generated and viewed in a real client: the four stages side by side (`uc-gallery-crop-stage0..3`), a field of mature plants and a close-up of the last stage. The first version had a stem that read as a green stick through the leaves; the stem was redrawn and the later frames show the nodes and the flower spike. It reads clearly as a serrated, multi-fingered plant and is deliberately fictional ("Sable Leaf", a controlled cultivar), consistent with the project's restricted-crop framing. Whether it is as realistic as the owner wants is a human call; if a better asset is licensed, it can replace ours without code changes (the stages are data).

## G. Founders Row v2 and the living world

A 25 x 41 lot, placed from data (808 operations): a registrar and a gate guard, a market with two vendors, a **Public Ledger**, five employers (farm co-op, **cellar**, foundry, gunsmith, network office), cottages with safes, a **vineyard plot**, a storehouse and a **firing range** (D-074: scored targets by hit position, a per-player best, an instructor, no payout). Twelve named citizens (five employers, two vendors, three residents, a registrar and a gate guard) are persistent villagers whose lines are data; a citizen you speak to stops and faces you, remembers a few things about you and goes back to their day (D-072). Frames viewed in a real client: the plaza, the cellar's front with its sign, the vineyard with Vinery's vines.

*What the scripted player found:* the first run **failed** at the Cellar Master and the Gunsmith's employer (`cursor MISS`, `cursor stone bricks`): the script stood a fixed 1.5 blocks toward the lane and was behind a counter or a wall. It now picks the nearest spot around the citizen with room to stand and a clear line to their head. The conversation steps that did run (registrar, vendor, guard, ledger) passed with the real mouse. With the fix the **whole scenario passes** (two runs, the second after the name-tag fix below): every citizen opened a conversation by real right click, the registrar and the gate guard were released after their conversation, and the ledger opened. The second run also showed a visual bug that the fixture frames could not: the game draws a named entity's floating name tag when it is under the crosshair, which a citizen you are speaking to is, so the tag was drawn across the portrait well and spilled over the panel's left edge. The dialogue screen now hides it while it draws the portrait, and the frame was looked at again.

## I. Firearms and the range

The Gun Smith Table is now guarded **on the server**: TaCZ Blueprints hides recipes in the client's screen only, so a modified client could build any gun. A narrow mixin on the table's `doCraft` reads the addon's own rule (its gating switch, always-enabled lists and the player's unlocked set) and refuses a craft the screen would not have offered (D-073). It was written and "tested" earlier, but **the mixin was never loaded** until this pass; with `--mixin.config` and `MixinConfigs` the GameTest "the Gun Smith Table refuses a gun without its blueprint" now passes against the real jars. The firing range scores hits through TaCZ's own event; the addon's default (`enableGunTable = false`, gated) is what the dev world uses. Not checked: the table's screen, a modified client, the addon's own screen.

## J. Vehicles and logistics

*New:* a car's crates are searched. Immersive Vehicles' cargo is not a `Container` but the inventory of crate parts; `VehicleCargo` reads them by reflection from the vehicle or the seat a rider is attached to and removes a seized stack with the mod's own call (D-077). GameTested with a **real Quad**, a real crate fitted by the mod's own part API and filled with a Sable Leaf Bale and wheat: when the Quad is brought into the zone the bale is seized, the wheat stays, the fine is charged; a parked Quad is not searched.

*Still open (not touched):* boat customs, survival fuel and a fuel source for cars (**research, October 18, read from the pinned Immersive Vehicles jar and not tried in a client:** the mod crafts a Jerrycan from iron ingots and a bucket, a Fuel Hose and a Fuel Pump (15,000 mB, 10 per tick) but registers no fuel fluid of its own, only the loaders and tanks that move fluids other mods provide; this pack has no such mod, so a survival car has no fuel source until one is chosen: a fluid or refinery mod, or a fuel and bucket that Core registers and a Smith or Engineer supply chain produces. That choice is the vehicle's running cost and belongs to the owner), transport contracts that need a vehicle, the Motorboat's upward-tilting camera and roadside settlements (road signage: see the October 18 update in section K).

## K. World quality and seed 5908

**Seed 5908 is unchanged**, and so are the three-city triangle, the roads and the bridges (no world-generation file differs from the October 16 head). The dev world still opens on the first launch and is never overwritten. This sprint did **not** improve roadside content or atmosphere. **Update, October 18 (D-078):** every road now has **waymarkers**: a stone-brick pillar with a lantern and a sign on each face every 250 blocks of open road, saying where the road leads, how far and how long on foot ("TO VALEWOOD / 740 m / 3 min on foot"). They are built at run time when a player comes near (once each, remembered by the world), so generation and the seed are untouched and existing worlds get them. GameTested (planning rules, a real pillar, both signs, persistence) and photographed in a real client on the Ironhaven - Valewood road (a scripted-player run read both signs and viewed the front, the back and the road). **Update, October 18 (D-079):** every road also gets **wayside shelters**: an open stop of one of two kinds that alternate along a road (a pavilion with a campfire, four seats, water and a cartography table, or a stone well under a small roof with two benches, a barrel and hay; two signs each) and a **road warden**, no more than 450 blocks apart and never within 150 blocks of a city, placed at run time like the markers on level, dry, unbuilt ground beside the road. The warden answers from real data: where the road leads and how long it takes on foot, the nearest checkpoint and what the law says at each end. A shelter has no economy. On the generated seed-5908 world a scripted player found five of the six planned spots buildable (Ironhaven - Valewood 2 of 2, Ironhaven - Blackwater 2 of 2, Valewood - Blackwater 1 of 2: the other had no level dry ground within 64 blocks and was given up), each decided in under 100 ms, and photographed some from the road and from under their roofs and talked to a warden. The first survey's photographs showed a well standing on a frozen sea (the ground scan stepped over water but stopped on ice); that is fixed, GameTested and re-surveyed with the same counts. A second client process then reloaded that saved world: the six decisions were remembered, three pavilions and two wells stood, and the warden was at its post (which also exposed a launcher gap: a reopened world waited at a vanilla dialog the launcher did not answer, now fixed). Roadside settlements with more than this (a trader, a story, an encounter) are still open (P1).
