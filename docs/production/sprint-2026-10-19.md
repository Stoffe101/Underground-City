## 3. Why Sophisticated Backpacks

A real configuration surface (slot counts and upgrade slots per tier, a switch for every item and its recipe, a per-type upgrade limit, a nerf for carrying many packs, a switch for mobs wearing packs), a placed pack that is a block with an item handler (so the Crew home guard sees it), and an active 2026 release line. Traveler's Backpack had no per-item switch and crashed one first start in eight. The cost is the licence (all rights reserved: runtime download only, like TaCZ). Details and the rejected alternatives: [backpack-economy.md](../technical/backpack-economy.md), [mod-audit.md](../technical/mod-audit.md).

## 4. How monster spawning is controlled

One rule in Core, three Forge events plus a conversion guard: a mob that is an `Enemy` or in the MONSTER category (of any mod, vanilla included) is refused at the position check (natural spawning and spawner blocks), at the finalise step (every spawn reason: structures, patrols, raids, reinforcements, jockeys, triggered spawns) and when it joins a level (which also removes hostile creatures an older world saved); lightning does not turn a villager or a pig into a monster. Permitted: the tag `uc_permitted_hostile` (scripted guards, armed criminals, future raids), the data tag `underground_city:permitted_hostiles`, a deliberate operator spawn. The game is **not** Peaceful and `doMobSpawning` is **not** switched off; hunger, thirst and hazards are untouched. The default lives in code, so a developer launch, a dedicated server and a single-player or LAN world behave the same; `hostileMobs = "CLASSIC"` in `world/serverconfig/underground_city-server.toml` is the documented opt-in. In Control! was read and not adopted (its rule file must exist in every game directory, otherwise the monsters silently return). The base game's own natural spawner is the witness in the tests, with a CLASSIC control that produces 93 and 70 monsters where the simulation produces none.

## 5. How monster-dependent resources stay obtainable

Gunpowder by TaCZ's own recipe (flint, sugar, charcoal); string, slime balls, spider eyes, ender pearls and blaze rods by finite, shared, slowly refilling exchange lines (pearls 120 credits and rods 200 credits, one per order, on a three to four unit shelf: rare on purpose, so the fifteen heavier TaCZ firearms stay scarce); blaze powder and magma cream by the base game's recipes from those; bones by cutting a ham. Everything else a hostile creature drops (rotten flesh, ghast tears, phantom membranes, nether stars, shulker shells and so on) is not needed by any approved gameplay, and the catalogue says so. A GameTest reads every loaded recipe and fails when an item of an approved mod needs a root without a route; a Python audit and a jar scan cover work orders, Core recipes and the vehicle packs. [monster-resources.md](../technical/monster-resources.md).

## 6. Backpack upgrades: available and restricted

Six tiers (27, 36, 45, 54, 72 and 90 slots with 1, 1, 2, 2, 3 and 4 upgrade slots, every one **at or below** the mod's defaults; the generator refuses a larger value). **Enabled (16 items):** the six packs, the upgrade base, Pickup, Filter, Magnet (range 3, worn pack only), Feeding (worn pack only), Restock, Deposit, Crafting, Jukebox and the first Stack upgrade (one per pack). **Switched off (55 items, each with a reason in `content/gameplay/backpack-policy.json`):** every higher stack tier, conversions, portable furnaces and other workstations, anvil, smithing, stonecutter, alchemy, void, infinity, inception, XP pump, battery, mob catcher, tanks and pumps (deferred), and every advanced variant. Server settings: nobody opens the pack another player wears, more than two packs slow you, only the worn pack runs upgrades, no shulker boxes or packs inside packs, no mobs wearing packs, no backpack loot in vanilla chests, the duplicate check stays on. The plain pack costs 70 credits in every city; bigger ones are made from materials. All numbers are first guesses for a person to judge.

## 7. Lost Cities performance findings

The long tick is the server thread parked in blocking chunk reads, from three callers: Lost Cities' queued sapling growth, the same task's own reads, and (vanilla) a villager's path recomputation. Longest tick of a 17 x 17 bulk request inside a city, Lost Cities 7.5.5: **4.9 to 10.9 s** by default (7 runs), plus a 30 to 38 s outlier in 2 of 10 runs. `forceSaplingGrowth = false` removes the first two (0.66 to 0.85 s in 8 runs); the navigation mixin removes the third; **both together: 0.60 to 0.80 s in 8 runs**. The price of the config line is visible: fewer grown city trees at generation (34.0k to 15.1k leaf blocks in the area measured), so it is flagged as an **owner decision** (D-096). A Core guard on tree growth had no effect and was removed. Other Lost Cities releases (7.5.6, 7.5.7) behave the same. The production watchdog is not disabled anywhere; bulk pregeneration on a production server is still not advised. Not shown: a person walking into a new city, two players. A draft upstream report exists and **was not posted**. [lost-cities-stalls.md](../technical/lost-cities-stalls.md).

## 8. Startup stability findings

38 server starts of the new mods in the cloud (15 clean first starts and 23 restarts, in three series: the survival set with and without the generated config, and every set together): **no failure, no crash report, no watchdog hit**. That does not prove the failure rate is lower than the retired mod's observed one in eight (15 clean starts would also occur 13 percent of the time at that rate); it is the same environment, one server at a time, idle. [first-start-stability.md](../technical/first-start-stability.md). The migration experiment found something more important: **a world saved with Traveler's Backpack and opened without it loses every Traveler's pack at the next save, with no question asked** (a backup restores them). Core now warns loudly before that happens; nothing converts anything. [backpack-migration.md](../technical/backpack-migration.md).

## 9. Test results (final code, cloud container)

* **GameTests:** **168 of 168** in each of six configurations (Core only; `firearms`; `vehicles`; `vintner,farming`; `survival`; every set at once), up from 139. Optional-mod tests pass trivially when their mod is absent, so only the configurations that load a mod execute its tests. **One flake:** in the first `firearms` run `theGunSmithTableRefusesAGunWithoutItsBlueprintOnTheServerAndBuildsItWithOne` failed ("A refused craft consumed nothing": the iron or copper count differed after a craft the Core gate had refused, as its log line shows); an immediate rerun of the same configuration passed 168 of 168 and the other five configurations never failed it. **The cause was not found** and the test was not changed. The navigation, legacy-notice, simulation, backpack and progression tests are among the 168; a mutation run showed the navigation test fails without its mixin.
* **Production-style server with every set** (reobfuscated Core jar, every pinned jar): `RESULT: pass` with `simulation = SIMULATION`, `backpack_policy = ok`, `monster_routes = ok`, `nav_unloaded_chunk = ok`, `boat_cargo = ok`.
* **Python:** 190 tests OK; repository validation, the backpack configuration check and the world package check pass; the item-source audit has 0 findings.
* **Combined journey** (generated Lost Cities world, every pack set, two real clients, the server killed without a stop sequence and restarted): **both phases passed**, with the Smith's real client buying, wearing, opening and filling a backpack on the real screens ([testing.md](../technical/testing.md)).
* **Hosted CI:** the last hosted run before this pass (head `b1e5e6d`) had three failures, all found and fixed here: the survival job still looked for Traveler's Backpack's log line; the journey clients did not get the generated backpack configuration (`prepareDevRun` refused); and a race in which the server halted before the member's client had recorded its last step. Results for the new head are in the PR checks (read after the push; see the PR for the current state).

## 11. Remaining issues and limitations

* No person has used any of it: not the backpack screens, not a night in the simulation world, not the exchange prices. The real-client part clicks through the screens' own handlers; it has no mouse pointer, no held shift key, and it exercises one pack of one player, not two clients opening the same pack.
* Not tested: any upgrade **in use through the client**. The GameTests insert, save and reload the Pickup upgrade, run the Magnet (in a placed pack) and the Feeding upgrade, and drive the Deposit and Restock packet gate; the Pickup, Filter, Crafting and Jukebox upgrades are otherwise checked as items that fit, persist and have recipes, not as behaviour. Also not tested: the mod's sort buttons, placing and breaking a pack with a real mouse, Windows, a real launcher account.
* A medical item is still a gap (backlog).
* The Lost Cities tree default, the 70-credit price and the 55 disabled upgrades are choices waiting for the owner's judgement.
* The probes show the mixin and the config remove the multi-second ticks **in a scripted bulk request**; whether a server with two players walking into new city stays under a second is unmeasured.
* The hosted CI state of the new head is not known when this is written; the PR page is the source of truth.
