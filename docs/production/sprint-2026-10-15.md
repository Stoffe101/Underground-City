# Sprint report, October 15-16, 2026

Branch `feature/professions-vehicles-firearms-sprint`, draft PR [#21](https://github.com/Stoffe101/Underground-City/pull/21) into `integration`. Nothing was merged. Everything below was built and tested in Claude's cloud container and GitHub-hosted runners, never on the owner's computer.

**Status vocabulary.** *implemented and tested* = code exists and an automated test that actually executes it passed (the test type is named). *implemented but not fully verified* = code exists, only part of it has run. *partial* = a slice, with the missing part named. *researched* = looked at, nothing built. *rejected* = looked at and ruled out, with the reason. *planned* = not built. A headless GameTest is never offered as proof of client behaviour, two-human play or fun.

## A. Executive summary

The starter slice now has a second half: every one of the four careers has a **legal path and an underground path on the same character**, with skill trees, 19 new contracts, city law, evidence-based checkpoints, buyer trust, heat, an interactive Technician trace, a fictional restricted crop (Sable Leaf) and non-farmable exceptional finds. The three cities are a **close triangle with a built road and bridge on every edge** (3.9 to 4.3 minutes on foot between outskirts, static estimate) on a new reference seed, 5908, whose roads were read block by block on a real dedicated server. Real **TaCZ**, **TaCZ Blueprints**, **Modern Life's Motorboat** and **Immersive Vehicles** with two official content packs boot together on a production-style Forge server with Core and Q Shop 1.8.4, in hosted CI. One operator-built district (**Founders Row**) exists. The terminal gained a Skills page and a Technician trace screen; all 33 integrated GUI surfaces were captured on a real client at 1920x1080 GUI 3 and 3440x1440 GUI 2 and the images were inspected twice, finding and fixing real defects.

What this is not: nobody has walked, driven or sailed any of it. There is no inhabited district, no guard NPC, no vehicle driving test, no client-driven test of the trace screen, and the balance is arithmetic over data. The slice is a stronger tested foundation, not a playable game.

## C. Geography

`scripts/plan_triangle_layout.py` derives the three city circles and the corridors from radii (600 / 410 / 570) and edge gaps (1,000 / 1,100 / 1,000); `cities.json` and `corridors.json` are generated and checked for drift. Seed **5908** won a two-stage scan (cheap climate pass over 30,000 seeds, then real surface and ocean-floor heights for the best 503) that scores dry city sites, 25-75% water on a ring 150 blocks outside each city so the cities read as islands or peninsulas, bridgeable water on each road, steep steps and the **height and relief of each city site**. Seed 579 was re-evaluated and rejected: with the new layout one city lies underwater. Seed 166 ranked first on water alone and was rejected after a hosted survey put Valewood on a 219-block frozen peak.

## G. Underground economy

Buyers have finite demand that replenishes only with active play, and a per-player trust level (maximum 5) gates the larger contracts. City law classifies each contraband category per city (`legal`, `permit`, `prohibited`) and sets inspection chances by gate risk. A search happens only in a checkpoint zone, from a seeded die; possession alone is never detected; zones can be walked around (documented smuggler's trail). Underground contracts pay up to about 2x the legal margin on paper and the validator keeps careless play at or below 0.95 of the legal baseline and hot play at or below 0.6. Status: implemented and tested as rules; **balance unproven by play**. No guards, no boat customs, no arrests.

## H. Loot

Rare loot is profession-wide, contextual and not farmable (D-053): household safes roll one exceptional find at registration from a position-fixed seed, real TaCZ guns keep their real NBT, rare components are packed items rather than currency. The datapack catalog `exceptional_loot` carries the table. Implemented and tested; a custom listener rather than a vanilla loot table because a table naming an absent mod's item fails to parse. Open: tuning the odds by playtest.

## K. What is playable now

On a fresh world with the pinned mods: register a citizen, place the terminal, run legal contracts for all four careers, learn skills, run underground contracts after the legal prerequisites, carry goods through checkpoint zones, face a seeded inspection, run the Technician trace at a secured terminal, grow Sable Leaf under lamps, build Founders Row as an operator. This is a tested mechanical slice. It is not a populated city, has no guards, no police, no inter-city travel content, and no one has played it for 30 minutes.
