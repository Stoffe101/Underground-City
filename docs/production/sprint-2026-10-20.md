## 2. The economic model and its assumptions

*The model is arithmetic over the game's data plus assumptions. It is not a measurement.* The assumptions (`content/gameplay/economy-model.json`): hands-on minutes per item, waiting time per crop (parallel, so it only delays a first delivery), a terminal visit and a share of an exchange trip per order, **only half of an active hour is hands-on** (walking, inventory, waiting), rent and living costs are not scaled by that, shared demand and shelves are spent once per household (a Crew of three of one career does not triple its income), and the household takes the best hour it can (an upper bound on cooperation). **Net** is credits minus cash spent at an exchange or shop; goods a player gathers or grows cost time. **Risk-adjusted underground returns** (`--underground`) deduct the checkpoint's seized goods, fines and heat from the same orders. The sensitivity table repeats the key outcomes with every minute scaled by 0.7 and 1.4; none of the findings reverses.

**Scenarios run:** five solo careers, farmer plus gunsmith, smith plus technician, a Crew of three (farmer, smith, gunsmith) and a Crew of three (vintner, technician, farmer). **Invariants that hold on the shipped data:** no buy-sell loop, no payout without a delivery, entry orders pay 63 to 77 percent per hands-on hour of the same career's other legal work, the ladder is monotonic, a purchase is 20 to 100 rents and a deposit two rents, no single sale buys a home outright, a payout skill cannot lift an order above the price of its goods, and the vehicle tiers match their stated wage hours.

| Farmhouse | 115 | 8,500 | no |
| Commercial workshop | 135 | 10,500 | no |
| Warehouse or production facility | 170 | 14,000 | no |
| Premium residence | 260 | 28,000 | no |

Billing cycle 60 active minutes; prepay up to six cycles; deposit two cycles, returned on a clean move-out; two cycles of grace, one of final notice, the fourth unpaid cycle lapses; sell-back 60 percent of the price paid; city factors Ironhaven 1.15, Blackwater 1.00, Valewood 0.90 (applied at signing); the first two motel hours are the city's welcome (once per person). **Rent never runs while all residents are offline and runs once however many are online.** A lapsed home loses building and workstations only; a move-out or sale is refused while it holds stored things. Containers per home are the listing's (5 in a flat, 10 in the house) plus the racks upgrade, enforced by the guard (the old fixed cap of four is gone).

## 5. Working skill capabilities

| Effect | Consumed by | Ceiling (node / tree) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `legal_payout_percent` | the credits a legal order pays, fixed into the prepared record at delivery | 6 / 12 |
| `underground_payout_percent` | the same for an underground order | 6 / 12 |
| `yield_percent` | extra Sable Leaf bales when a player harvests a mature plant | 30 / 40 |
| `upgrade_discount_percent` | the acting member's price of a home upgrade | 15 / 20 |
| `exchange_discount`, `inspection_modifier`, `licences` | the exchange, the checkpoint (unchanged) | unchanged |

Legal and underground lines are both fully reachable by level 30 (25 to 29 of 29 points); the legal line and the foundation never need a crime; a crossover needs one legal and one underground parent. **Respec does not exist** (no infinite respec). The Farmer's Sable Leaf production loop is the one complete production-to-profit chain: buy catalyst and seed stock at the exchange, craft nutrient and cuttings, plant (needs the controlled-cultivation skill), feed, harvest (extra bales from the grafting skill), deliver to a licensed buyer for legal pay or to Marrow for the underground rate. Payouts were rebalanced; the table of changes is in [economy-model.md](../technical/economy-model.md).

## 6. Police and Sophisticated Backpacks

See D-100 and [checkpoint-search.md](../technical/checkpoint-search.md). Closed: a worn pack, an armour slot, a pack or a shulker box in a pocket and a pack in a ridden vehicle's hold. Legal goods inside the same pack are never taken (tests). ## 8. Vehicles (D-101)

The audit read the running mods: Immersive Vehicles registers every item of its packs in one builder class (244 vehicle items; cars are built at the Vehicle Bench from pack-defined parts that were not priced); Modern Life's bicycle and motorboat have vanilla recipes. The exchange sells the bicycle in every city and the motorboat in Valewood from small shared stock. Aircraft, a tank and bombers are not offered; whether the Bench may build the packs' weapon parts is an **open owner decision**. Fuel, repair, insurance, parking and driving were **not** implemented or tested.
