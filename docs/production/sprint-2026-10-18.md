## C. Crews

**Architecture and financial authority.** Q Shop 1.8.4 stays the only currency; it has no group concept, so Core owns a ledger (`CrewLedger`, one atomic fsynced file `data/underground_city_crews.dat`) of credits that have *left* a wallet. Every transfer is: validate, write a prepare record (with the wallet's before and after values), set the wallet to the recorded absolute value, save and read back the player file, then settle the ledger and clear the prepare record in one write. Recovery at server start and at login compares the wallet with the recorded values and only ever reads it. Details and the evidence table: [crew-economy.md](../technical/crew-economy.md). Why no party mod owns this: [mod-audit.md](../technical/mod-audit.md#october-18-candidate-review-crews-parties-shared-money-and-the-survival-set).

**Features that exist** (each is named in [crews.md](../design/crews.md) with its test): create, invite (online registered citizens), accept or decline, roles and a permission table the founder edits, kick below your rank, leave, hand over, claim leadership after 30 days of founder absence, disband (two steps, typed name, bank split as owed payouts, homes pass to the founder), rename; deposit by anyone, withdraw and spend by permission, history, three finance modes with consent; leasable homes with a showroom state, a city-owned shell, private containers with a three-day retrieval window; the Crew screen (Crew, Money, Home, Rules pages) with tooltips that say why a button is disabled.

## F. The Gunsmith's component loop

Audit first: the chain "found or bought components, a blueprint, the Gun Smith Table, a contract that buys the result, XP, a skill, a better contract" already existed in pieces ([item-source-audit.md](../technical/item-source-audit.md)). The audit (`scripts/audit_item_sources.py`, nine unit tests) reads every work order against every kit, exchange line, crop, recipe, reward and loot table and found **one real dead end**: the AK-47 and M4A1 of the level-11 `arms_brokerage` contract can only be built with a blueprint (D-073), and the only sources were rolled loot. **Fix:** an underground errand `dray_rifle_run` (level 10, Filed Serials, buyer Dray, trust 1) takes six machined parts and three optical components (both bought at the regional exchange, which means visiting another city) and pays 330 credits, 450 XP and the **AK-47 blueprint**. It passes the contract generator's margin and reward-value rules and the balance validator.

**A second dead end, found by the test written to prove the fix:** my first choice was the M4A1 blueprint, and the chain GameTest failed at the last step. TaCZ's own table recipe for the M4A1 **fits a stock**, and a contract takes only *plain* factory guns (delivery consumes the item), so a crafted M4A1 can never be delivered. The data audit cannot see TaCZ's recipes; the reward is now the AK-47 (plain from the table), and a GameTest now checks, with TaCZ loaded, that every gun requirement of every contract lists at least one gun the table builds plain.

**The chain, as one GameTest on real TaCZ and TaCZ Blueprints** (`theRestrictedRifleChainRunsFromExchangePartsThroughTheBlueprintAndTheTableToPaymentAndTrust`, firearms set): ten machined parts bought in Ironhaven and five optical components in Blackwater (two rival cities' shared stock goes down by exactly that; the credits spent are exactly the unit prices), Dray's first run (trust 1, the SMG blueprint), the rifle run (trust 2, the AK-47 blueprint, no trust means no accept), the blueprint-gated table refusing the rifle until the blueprint is used and then building it from 38 iron, 6 lapis and 10 logs, the level-11 contract buying the crafted rifle (trust 3), the three contracts' XP exactly as listed and every credit accounted for. What it is **not**: the table's screen, a rifle fired, how long this takes a person, or whether the prices feel right.

**Research, not built (a Core-owned TaCZ gun pack).** TaCZ 1.1.8 reads gun recipes from gun packs (`data/<namespace>/recipes/gun/*.json`, type `tacz:gun_smith_table_crafting`, `materials` with items or tags and counts; the default Glock 17 is sixteen iron ingots) and has a `recipe_filters` loader with `whitelist` and `blacklist` entries. A Core-owned pack could therefore add a recipe whose materials include Core components and filter the default one out. That would make components part of the gun itself. It is **unverified** on a dedicated server with TaCZ Blueprints and the blueprint gate, needs a pack folder prepared at launch, and is the next step if the owner wants it ([expansion-plan.md](expansion-plan.md), WP5 and WP6).

## G. The previous engineering issues

| Issue | Finding and action |
| --- | --- |
| Concurrent login `ConcurrentModificationException`, cause unproven | **Still unproven and not reproduced.** Review: every Core packet handler (`TerminalNetwork`, `CrewNetwork`) hands its work to the server thread with `enqueueWork`; Core registers no login-phase packet and subscribes no handler on the network thread; the statics that are not thread-safe (`WeakHashMap`s keyed by player) are touched only from server-thread work. The stack trace was never kept, so no line of ours can be named. What changed: nothing is hidden any more (below) |
| Login retry in the scripted client | The retry stays (it is bounded to three attempts and only before the player is in) but is now **recorded and surfaced**: each client writes `journey-client-connection.json` (attempts, the disconnect text of every dropped login), the runner puts it in `report.json` and prints a `::warning::` annotation for any run that needed a retry, so a run with a retry is visibly different from a clean one. In the final Crew journey both clients needed one attempt and no login was dropped. |
| Shelter placement and interrupted-build recovery | Fixed: [D-086](decisions.md). The record was written before the build and a failed build was filed as "given up for lack of ground" for ever; a record that the disk never received hid a missing shelter for ever. Now failures are counted and retried, then reported until `/uc_shelters retry`; a recorded shelter is verified against the world once per start; a lot that lost more than half of its blocks is removed properly and rebuilt; a missing warden is put back; an operator's `/uc_district undo` is respected. GameTested with an injected mid-build fault, a wiped lot, a few missing blocks, an undo and a deleted warden |
| Persistence, restart and reload | The Crew journey is the first test that kills the dedicated server process and restarts it with players and money in flight; the work-order ledger and districts had restart tests before. Not tested: a reload with the Lost Cities generator and the full pack, and a long session |

## I. Farming and brewing mods in the default pack

Vinery, Farm & Charm and Kaleidoscope Tavern stay **opt-in** (`vintner`, `farming`). Reasons: their licences are "custom" and could not be read in the cloud; Farm & Charm overlaps the survival kitchen (Farmer's Delight); a production-chain test against Core's economy (do their wines feed `tavern_table_wine` and `estate_cellar` orders in a running pack, and does a barrel survive a restart) exists only as the GameTests and a boot, not as a played chain. The audit lists their goods as "external" for exactly this reason. Farmer and Vintner remain separate careers. A default-pack decision needs a played production chain; that is a sprint, not a switch.

## J. Known issues and risks

* The Crew screen refreshes every five seconds; a mate's action can take that long to appear (acceptable for two friends, to be judged by a person).
* Fully shared finances cannot route Q Shop's own shop screens.
* The journey's world is flat and thin; the Row Flats stand on a stone mesa there. It proves the money, the screen and the restart, not the Lost Cities terrain.
* `Runtime.halt` is a stand-in for `kill -9` and a power cut; disk write ordering was designed for the latter (fsync of the file and the directory) but only the former is tested.
* The survival set is not certified with every other pack mod at once beyond the boot records.
* All counts and timings are from a four-core cloud container with software OpenGL.
